Sway with nvidia driver
============================
Sway will fail to if your running the proietry nvidia drivers, as it's unsupported. So when you attempt to jump into sway it'll just die and go back to GDM.
Looking at the journalctl it'll say it's unsupported and you need to add '--unsupported-gpu'. So let's do that:

  $ cat /usr/share/wayland-sessions/sway.desktop
  [Desktop Entry]
  Name=Sway
  Comment=An i3-compatible Wayland compositor
  Exec=sway --unsupported-gpu
  Type=Application
